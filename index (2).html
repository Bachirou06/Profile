Profile

I am a highly dedicated Renewable Energy Engineer with a strong focus on energy planning, renewable potential assessment and electrification planning using a geospatial approach. I have supported electrification planning initiatives and government institutions through the development of geospatial tools, rural electrification and demand forecasting models. I use my analytical skills to conduct rigorous spatial techno-economic feasibility and optimization studies, integrating geospatial data to inform effective project design and implementation. I have contributed to numerous projects across Sub-Saharan Africa, supporting long-term energy planning and electricity access, checking the viability of mini-grid systems, and building the capacity of local experts in energy system modeling and analysis.
